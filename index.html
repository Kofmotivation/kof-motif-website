                About

                Kofi started experimenting with his creativity in Ghana at Prempeh College, where he studied Graphic Design. Before he was set to graduate, he migrated to the USA in 2012 to continue his education in Art and Design. He later received his Design Degree from Michael Graves College, formerly known as Kean University, and began his postgraduate path at a tech start-up in Brooklyn building AI-powered mobile applications— “a very dope way to start my professional career.”

                Through all of that, he felt he simply couldn’t afford to bring his camera along with him. He is now working in fashion photography with a disruptive and unapologetic style, while continuing in graphic design and beginning to venture into the cinema space. Based between New York City, Los Angeles, and Ghana.

                Sound

                Kof Motif is a recording artist blending alternative R&B with subtle Afrobeat textures. His debut album, Renaissance Cruise, is an 11-track exhale—intimate, unhurried, and quietly confident. Written for the moments after a long day, it pairs soft, honest vocals with warm, restrained production.

                Focus

                Photography across fashion campaigns, editorial portraits, product, brand content, and creative direction— from first visual concept through final retouched assets.

                Approach: concept development, lighting and styling, retouching and delivery, visual storytelling, emotional honesty, intention over trend.
