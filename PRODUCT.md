# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (existing codebase; no framework)

## Users

Art directors, creative directors, music listeners, and commissioning clients who browse Kof Motif’s stills, motion, music, diary, and shop — with hire/contact as the primary professional close.

## Product Purpose

A multi-discipline creative portfolio for Kof Motif. Success means a visitor can explore Work collections, Motion, Music, Diary, and Shop, then email to inquire about hiring or purchases.

## Positioning

An image-first creative house — stills lead, with motion, music, diary, and shop as peer surfaces. Named Work collections open into galleries (Oshane Howard–style project depth). Craft ambition: Awwwards-level motion, preloader, and transitions within a photographer-portfolio grammar.

## Operating Context

Cinematic loader → Work (collection covers). Menu opens Work / Motion / Music / Diary / Shop / About. Clicking a Work cover opens a collection gallery (placeholder sets until real series are added). Contact remains `kofmotivation@gmail.com`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Work: mosaic of collection covers; each opens a multi-image collection view (placeholder groupings until user updates).
- Motion, Music, Diary, Shop: peer tabs with placeholder imagery from `assets/work/`.
- Primary hire action: About → email.
- Shop is a placeholder merch surface (no real checkout yet).
- Do not invent client, publication, or commission claims.
- Contact: `kofmotivation@gmail.com` (temporary).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Kof Motif**
- Binding craft references: markclennon.com, siemprericc.com, gregorcollienne.com, oshanehoward.com (collections + menu depth), Awwwards SOTD photography portfolios (preloader, transitions, microinteractions).
- Visual world remains black ground, white chrome, Bodoni Moda + Archivo — not Oshane’s yellow/green palette.

## Evidence on Hand

- Work Series 1–29 stills imported from Desktop `KOFI DUAH WEBSITE/WORK` into `assets/work/series-XX/` (Series 22 and 30 folders were empty).
- No real client list, press quotes, or completed shop inventory.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; chrome stays quiet.
2. Collections, not orphan stills, are the Work unit.
3. Never fabricate commissions or clients.
4. Peer surfaces (Motion / Music / Diary / Shop) share one grammar.
5. Motion and loading are materials of the site, not decoration.
